What is My Budget Deal 99?

We are building a content-first destination for people who want practical products, useful gadgets and budget-friendly buying ideas.

Our goal is simple: research products, explain what they are useful for, highlight important things to consider before buying, and help readers make more informed purchasing decisions. We focus on value, usefulness and honest recommendations rather than simply promoting products.
